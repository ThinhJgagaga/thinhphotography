A camera as a way of paying attention.

Photography began for me as a way to hold on to small, fleeting things: the light on a familiar street, the energy of a school day, a city revealing a different side of itself. Now I use it to make space for those moments and let them speak in their own voice.

My work moves between documentary observation and a love of considered composition. Whether I'm photographing people, places, or machines, I'm looking for the detail that makes an image feel lived in.
